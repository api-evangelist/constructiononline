Labor is one of the largest costs on any construction project—often 30% to 50% of total project cost for self-performed work, and for some trades and contract types, more. It's also one of the costs most likely to be wrong in the estimate, for a simple reason: the number most builders carry for labor isn't the number labor actually costs. Materials get quoted.
