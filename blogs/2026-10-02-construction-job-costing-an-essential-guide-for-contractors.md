When it comes to construction project management, the estimate only tells a small part of the financial story. What affects your profits is the actual costs incurred as the project progresses. This complete guide will walk you through the ins and outs of construction job costing, including the crucial role it plays in protecting profits, the hidden job costing errors that erode margins, and best practices for implementing accurate job costing.
