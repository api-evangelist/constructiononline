Every experienced builder knows the feeling. A change comes up mid-project—a client wants a different countertop, an inspector flags a code update, a subcontractor hits a hidden condition behind a wall—and suddenly there's a flurry of phone calls, emails, sticky notes, and "I'll get to it later" promises. The work moves forward, but the documentation lags behind.
